layout

the big boxes a page is made of
luca baggi.nav · search · toggle

header

also: top bar, navbar, site header

The strip across the top of every page. It holds the wordmark, the nav and site-wide controls. If it stays pinned while you scroll, it is a sticky header.

on your site: header.top in the base layout. Yours scrolls away with the page.

luca baggi.

wordmark

also: logo, brand, logotype

A logo made only of text. A logo that is a symbol is a logomark; the little picture in the browser tab is the favicon.

on your site: "luca baggi" with the orange full stop, top left.

hi, i'm luca 👋product-minded data scientist…

hero

also: hero section, banner, intro

The large opening block at the top of a page that says what the page is. What fits on screen before any scrolling is above the fold.

on your site: the "hi, i'm luca" block on the home page (.intro).

grid & columns

also: two-column layout, sidebar (when one column is narrow and secondary)

How content is split side by side. Columns can have a fixed ratio (yours is about 3 : 2) and usually stack into one column on phones.

on your site: posts and tidbits on the home page (.streams); the date column on the talks page.

max-width

container

also: wrapper, content width, max-width

An invisible box that caps how wide the content can get and centres it, so lines don't stretch across a wide monitor.

on your site: .wrap caps the page at 1100px; post text is narrower (560 to 760px).

gutter

also: side padding, page margin

The empty space between the content and the edge of the screen (the shaded bands). "Gutter" is also used for the gap between columns.

on your site: 32px on each side (--gutter).

also: section title; the small line next to it is a tagline or subhead; a tiny label above a title is an eyebrow or kicker

The heading that opens a section of a page, often with a short note and a rule under it.

on your site: "posts · the long ones. grab a coffee." (.s-head).

nav links

The links to the top-level pages. When they look like a row of switchable labels they are often called tabs, even though each one loads a new page. Click one in the specimen.

active state

also: current page indicator, selected state; your specific style is a highlighter or marker underline

How the UI shows "you are here". Common versions: an underline bar, a filled background, bold text, or a highlighter stroke like yours, which is a very thick, low underline in the pastel --mark colour.

on your site: the active nav tab, the active talks view, and the rotating word in your bio all share it.

view switcher

also: tabs (top), segmented control (bottom), toggle group

Shows the same content in different ways without leaving the page. Drawn as words it is a set of tabs; drawn as one rounded track with a sliding selected segment it is a segmented control (the iOS name).

on your site: map / by talk / timeline. It used to be pills; it is now tabs with the highlighter.

breadcrumb

A small path above a page title that shows where the page sits and links back up the tree.

on your site: "writing / posts" above every post title (.crumb).

text link

also: inline link, anchor

Clickable words inside text. A link that jumps to a spot on the same page (#map) is an anchor link or hash link; one that opens another site usually carries a ↗ arrow.

on your site: the orange underlined slides / video / code links on the talks page.

skip to content

skip link

also: skip to content

A hidden link that appears only when you press Tab, so keyboard and screen-reader users can jump past the header straight to the content.

on your site: press Tab on any page of the blog and it appears top left.

controls

things you press, type into, or flip

button

variants: primary (filled, the main action), secondary or outline, ghost or text button (no border until hover); the main action on a marketing page is the CTA (call to action)

Does something. A link goes somewhere; a button acts. A pill-shaped button is still a button, just with fully rounded ends.

on your site: mostly ghost-style: "copy page", the code block's wrap and copy.

icon button

also: icon-only button; the round grey background on hover is the hover state or hit area

A button with only an icon and no visible label. It still needs a hidden label for screen readers (aria-label). The clickable area is the hit area or tap target, ideally at least 44px on phones.

on your site: the moon / sun button in the header.

try it: flips this page

toggle

also: theme toggle, dark mode switch; the sliding pill version is a switch

A control with two states that flips between them. A button that stays "on" after you press it is a toggle button (or pressed state); the code block's "wrap" is one.

on your site: the theme toggle in the header; "wrap" on code blocks.

split button & dropdown menu

also: the arrow is a chevron or caret; the list is a dropdown, menu or popover menu; each line is a menu item

A split button has a main action on the left and a chevron on the right that opens more options. Open it in the specimen. The small grey second line in each item is its description or helper text.

on your site: "copy page" on every post (.cp, .cp-menu).

search trigger

also: search button; a real typing box is a search field or search bar

Yours looks like a search box but is a button: clicking it opens the command palette, where the typing happens.

on your site: the "search ⌘K" pill in the header. On phones it turns into a plain box with a magnifier.

keyboard hint

also: shortcut hint, key cap, kbd (after the HTML tag)

A little key drawing that tells you which key does what.

on your site: ⌘K in the header, and the ↑↓ move · ↵ open · esc close row at the bottom of search.

input

also: text field, form field; the grey hint inside is the placeholder

A box you type into. Click it to see its focus ring (the coloured glow), which shows where the keyboard is.

on your site: the box at the top of the search palette.

Describes a shape more than a job: anything with fully rounded ends. A small pill next to a title that says what kind of thing it is gets called a tag or label.

modal & command palette

also: dialog, overlay; the dimmed layer behind is the backdrop or scrim; the ⌘K flavour is a command palette (Spotlight on a Mac)

A modal is a box on top of the page that blocks the rest until you close it. A command palette is a modal built around one input and a list of results, driven from the keyboard. The highlighted row is the selected result.

on your site: search (.search-modal, .search-box).

also: popup, floating panel

A small panel attached to the thing that opened it. Unlike a modal it doesn't dim the page, and clicking elsewhere closes it. Dropdown menus are a kind of popover.

on your site: the city card that opens on the talks map; the copy page menu.

padding, border, margin

together: the box model; all the empty space: whitespace or breathing room

From the inside out: padding is space inside the border (grey), the border is the line, margin is space outside it (hatched). "Tighter" means less space, "looser" or "airier" means more.

display heading

also: headline, H1; the font for it is the display face

The big title. Headings go H1 (biggest) to H6, and the difference in size and weight between them is the type hierarchy. Weight is boldness: regular 400, medium 500, semibold 600, bold 700, extra bold 800.

on your site: Bricolage Grotesque at weight 800 for headings; Instrument Sans for text.

tracking & leading

CSS names: letter-spacing and line-height; kerning is spacing between one specific pair of letters

Tracking is the space between all letters; big headings usually get tighter tracking. Leading (rhymes with "wedding") is the space between lines.

on your site: headings are tracked tight (−0.05em); body text has loose leading (1.65).

about 45 to 75 characters a line reads best on screen

measure

also: line length, reading width

How many characters fit on a line of body text. Too wide and the eye loses its place at each line break.

on your site: post text is held to 560 to 760px.

rule

also: divider, separator, hairline (when 1px and faint)

A thin line that separates sections or rows.

How rounded the corners are: square, slightly rounded, or fully round (a pill or circle).

also: shadow, drop shadow, depth; no shadow at all is flat

A shadow makes something look raised above the page. Higher elevation means a bigger, softer shadow and suggests the element floats on top.

giving feedback

a recipe that works

Name the element, the state it's in, the property you want changed, and the direction. Where it happens (desktop, phone, dark mode) saves a round of questions.
